# Item Previewer

Try on any clothing item in Animal Jam Classic. Only you can see it: the item is shown by your own game, and nothing is sent to the server.

## How to use

1. Open **Item Previewer** from the sidebar.
2. Put on or take off any item in the game once, so the plugin knows which animal you're on.
3. Search for an item, pick it, choose a color number, enter its position in your own inventory, and press **Try it on** (or double-click the item).

The inventory number labels the preview for your mapping. It does not become a game or packet slot; previews continue to use separate temporary slots so they cannot collide with real inventory items.

Preview items also appear in your clothing inventory, so you can put them on and take them off from the game like real items.

## Notes

- Keep the window open while previewing. Closing it takes the preview items off.
- Preview items stay listed in your inventory until you log in again, but they can't be recycled, traded or kept.
- Changing rooms shows your real outfit again. With **Keep on when changing rooms** checked, previews are put back on automatically.
- Switch animals? Put on or take off any item once on the new animal.
